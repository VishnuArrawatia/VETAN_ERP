4. Employee Master

The Employee Master is where you manage all employee records.

Step 1: Open Employee Master

1 Click "Employee Master" in the left sidebar

Employee Master Features

1 Search Box — Type Employee ID, Name, or Phone to find any employee

2 Company Filter — Select a specific company/unit to view only those employees

3 Add Employee — Click to add a new employee record

4 Import Excel — Bulk import employees from Excel

5 Export Excel — Download employee list as Excel

6 Edit Button — Click on employee row to modify record

Step 2: Add New Employee

1 Click "+ Add Employee" button
2 Fill in mandatory fields: Employee ID, Name, Company
3 Fill optional fields: Department, Designation, Phone, Date of Joining
4 Enter salary details: Base Salary, Salary Structure (FIXED)
5 Click "Save Employee"
Employee ID is PERMANENT. Do NOT change it after creation. The system generates the password from Employee ID automatically.

7. Payroll Processor

Calculate monthly salary for all employees.

Step 1: Open Payroll Processor

1 Click "Payroll Processor" in the left sidebar

Step 2: Select Month & Company

2 Select Month (e.g., May 2026)
3 Select Company (SVN-1, Sakar-I, or ALL)

Step 3: Calculate Salary

4 Click "Calculate Draft Salary"

What System Auto-Calculates

Education AllowanceOther Deductions

Step 4: Download & Verify

5 Click "Download Excel" to export salary sheet
6 Verify all amounts are correct

Step 5: Close Payroll

7 Click "Close & Approve Payroll" to lock the month
After closing payroll, no further changes are allowed for that month. Make sure all data is correct before closing.

8. Salary Revision

Process increments and salary changes.

Step 1: Open Salary Revision

1 Click "Salary Revision" in the left sidebar

Step 2: Search Employee

2 Enter Employee ID or Name to find the employee

Step 3: Enter New Salary

3 Current salary is auto-filled (read-only)
4 Enter New Base Salary amount
5 Select Effective From Date (e.g., 01-May-2026)
6 Enter Reason (e.g., "May-26 Increment")
7 Click "Save Revision"
Salary Revision does NOT overwrite the old salary. The system maintains a complete history of all salary changes with dates and reasons.

9. Loan Management

Track employee loans and auto-deduct EMI from salary.

Step 1: Open Loan Management

1 Click "Loan Management" in the left sidebar

Step 2: Add New Loan

2 Click "Add New Loan" tab
3 Select Employee
4 Enter Loan Amount and Monthly EMI
5 Click "Save Loan"
Loan EMI is automatically deducted during payroll calculation. When HR runs "Calculate Draft Salary", the system checks for active loans and deducts the EMI from net pay.

12. Troubleshooting

ProblemSolution
"Authentication service unavailable" Internet connection problem. Check WiFi/mobile data. Try again after 30 seconds.
"Incorrect Password" Check Employee ID and Password. SVN: Phone last 4 + Birth year. Sakar: 00001995
"Employee not found" Check Employee ID spelling. It is case-sensitive. Contact HR if problem persists.
"Failed to fetch" Server is temporarily unavailable. Wait 1-2 minutes and try again.
Page not loading / Blank screen Clear browser cache: Chrome → Settings → Clear browsing data → Cached images and files.
Cannot see other company data Check your access level. HR users can only see their assigned unit.
Salary revision not saving Payroll month may be locked. Contact Super Admin to unlock.
Attendance not updating Click "Save Attendance" button after making changes.
